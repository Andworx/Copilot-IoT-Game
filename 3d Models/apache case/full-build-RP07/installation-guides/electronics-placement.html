Electronics placement & wire routing

Mechanical installation map for the external 12 V architecture, recessed fuse block, two Raspberry Pi 4Bs and three DC-to-5 V converters. RP07 replaces the hidden converter drops with one accessible shared slot. Coordinates are measured from the case center; front is negative Y and the physical case floor is Z0.

Installation candidate: this page is a routing and placement guide, not a final electrical schematic. Keep the external supply disconnected while assembling. Confirm polarity, switch pinout, fuse sizes, conductor gauge, terminal insulation and the selected 5 V power topology before energizing.

Positive route: XT60 positive → verified master-switch terminals → fuse-block positive input → one correctly sized branch fuse per converter/load.
Negative route: XT60 negative → fuse-block negative bus → individual negative returns to every converter/load. Do not use printed structure or random hardware as a current return.
Control route: Dial, panel switches, activity sockets and LEDs go to low-current GPIO/driver circuits through labeled disconnects. Do not route load current through GPIO or the selector.

Power-topology gate: decide how the official display and its attached Pi receive 5 V before wiring. Do not feed the same 5 V rail through multiple power connectors or jumper paths unless the exact configuration has been reviewed for backfeed.

Assembly checklist

  • Pass the RP07 converter-bay coupon with all three converters and their real leads before printing the full left tray.
  • Install and verify M3 heat-set inserts before loading the tray.
  • Use M3 × 8 mm at tray-only bosses and trial M3 × 12 mm through tray joiners; confirm engagement without bottoming.
  • Mount fuse block and converters before placing the tray if lower fasteners become inaccessible.
  • Add abrasion protection at every tray and case crossing.
  • Keep all wires below the fixed panel and away from lid/hinge movement.
  • Label both ends of every harness before bundling.
  • Continuity-test polarity with the external supply disconnected.
  • Energize through a current-limited test setup after fuse and conductor selection.
