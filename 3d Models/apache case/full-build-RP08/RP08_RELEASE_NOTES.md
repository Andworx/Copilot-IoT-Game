# RP08 release notes - approved controls and measured rear recess

RP08 promotes the physically approved T02-E power-switch opening (23.6 x
29.8 mm), the T02-P3 dial shoulder pocket (diameter 19.0 mm, depth 2.0 mm),
and the physically approved RP07 converter layout with its 12 x 42 mm shared
power-drop slit.

The measured rear flat recess is 132.55 x 75.62 mm. The rear carrier is now
128 x 60 mm, centered in that recess, with model control centers at X -30 for
the dial, X +1 for the master switch, and X +36 for the XT60 inlet. The carrier
mounts move to X +/-59 and Z 24/66. A 7.5-10.5 mm register-pin slot is retained
because the passing dial pocket was identifiable but the exact pin tick was
not readable.

The case drill guide uses 38 x 40 mm XT60, 30 x 36 mm switch, and diameter
31 mm dial service openings. Only the four 3.5 mm carrier mounting holes are
drilled separately; XT60 fasteners attach to the printed carrier.

Changed production STLs relative to RP07 are `carrier.stl`,
`layout_template.stl`, `rear_L.stl`, `rear_R.stl`, and `tray_L.stl`. All other
production STLs are copied forward byte-identically.
